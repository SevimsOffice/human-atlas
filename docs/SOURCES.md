## Derived values (shown on the in-app Sources page)
- **O₂ uptake at 70 kg:** College Physics VO2 (76-kg male) → mL/kg/min → × 70 kg (A&P reference body). Linear per-kg scaling is an assumption.
- **O₂ taken from blood:** VO2(70 kg) ÷ CO, labelled "derived (Fick principle)"; formula citation pending.
- **Organ blood flow:** organ share of the A&P §20.4 column × engine CO; running uses the "maximal exercise" column.
